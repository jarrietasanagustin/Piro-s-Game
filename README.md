# Piro's Game

**[▶ Play it](https://jarrietasanagustin.github.io/piros-game/)** — works in any
browser, phone or desktop.

A small spheroid sits in the Stokes flow between two eccentric cylinders (a
journal bearing). You rotate the outer cylinder, then the inner one, then undo
both. Every cylinder ends exactly where it began — but the particle does not.
It has drifted, and turned.

That residue is a **geometric phase**: the flow is inertialess and
rate-independent, so nothing depends on how fast you turn the cylinders, only on
the loop you trace in the space of cylinder angles. Loops enclosing no area give
nothing back. The particle's position and orientation are the holonomy of that
loop.

Your job is to exploit it: drive the spheroid onto a target position **and**
orientation. Both at once is the hard part.

## Rules

- 20 cycles. Stop earlier if you like.
- Nobody wins. You are scored on how far off you left the spheroid,
  in position and in orientation — lower is better, and under 1.00 means you
  landed inside both tolerances.
- Everyone plays the same configuration: same start, same target,
  ε = 0.4, aspect ratio 5, R₁ = 1, R₂ = 0.3.
