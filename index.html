Piro's Game

a geometric-phase toy

Drive a tiny spheroid through a Stokes flow by rotating two cylinders.

Rotating both back to start should return everything home — but it doesn't. The particle drifts, and rotates. That stubborn residue is the geometric phase.

Your job: dial a loop, run it, repeat. Try to land both position and orientation on the target.

You get 20 cycles, and nobody wins. Stop when you like, and you are told exactly how far off you left it. Lower score, better driving.
